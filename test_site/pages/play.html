The Checkout Challenge

Get through checkout on this test shop without being flagged as automated. Come as yourself, write a script, or run an agent — see if you slip through.

All you have to do:

  • Add a product to your cart
  • Go to checkout
  • Fill in the order form with anything plausible
  • Submit the order

Once your order's confirmed, we'll show you our best guess at what you were — then you tell us what you actually were.

Start the challenge →

This is a research project on detecting automated (bot/agent) web traffic. Playing captures mouse movement, click, and keystroke timing, plus browser fingerprint data (not keystroke content) — no other personal information. Data is used for bot-detection research only.
