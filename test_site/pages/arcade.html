Research project on detecting automated (bot/agent) web traffic. Playing captures mouse/click/keystroke timing and browser fingerprint data (never keystroke content). Name and email at the end are optional and used only for the leaderboard / occasional updates — email is never shown publicly.

Prove you're human. Or don't.

Play through a quick gauntlet of mini-games — reaction, perception, memory, precision. When you're done, we'll guess whether you were a human, a bot script, or an AI agent. Then you tell us which one you actually were.

Aim for a high score — getting a challenge wrong doesn't just skip it, it costs points and sends you back to try again, so going fast and guessing isn't actually faster.

  • ~7 short stages, most under 10 seconds each
  • No sign-up required to play
  • Score goes on the leaderboard for your category
  • Wrong answers cost points and reset that challenge — no free passes
  • Human — a person playing normally
  • Bot / script — code on a fixed script, no on-screen reasoning
  • AI agent — an LLM deciding each move by looking at the page
