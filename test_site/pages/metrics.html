Detection rates

In the real world there are exactly three things a visitor can be: Human, Bot (scripted/mechanical automation), or Agent (LLM-driven automation). Our own generator scripts and the scanner writeup give us that ground truth here; for a real, unlabeled visitor the score can only ever say automation-or-not (coarse_verdict is Bot/Human) — telling Bot from Agent for them requires their own self-report. This section shows, for each of the three, the fraction where coarse_verdict (overall_detection_score ≥ 50, the exact number a player's reveal screen shows) reads "Bot" — the catch rate for Bot/Agent, the false-positive rate for Human. Band (block/step_up/allow) is the finer-grained breakdown behind the same score, shown for context.

Recorded accuracy

Every /api/reveal writes a detection_accuracy event — correct calls included, not just misses, human self-reports included, not just bots/agents. This is a persisted log of what the verdict actually said AT THE TIME, using whatever weights were live then — unlike Detection Rates above, which re-scores everything with CURRENT weights right now. The two will drift apart as weights adapt; that gap is itself a signal of how much the live system has moved.
